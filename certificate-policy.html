Certificate policy

Version 2026-08-14

What is awarded

A signed-in learner becomes eligible after every module in one path is recorded complete: 18 modules for Java, Docker, or Python, or 12 modules for Generative AI Foundations, RAG Systems, or Agentic AI. The learner must then choose a public display name, review the disclosure, and explicitly claim and publish the certificate.

What it demonstrates

The certificate demonstrates recorded completion of the named QuickDevBase path and its listed concepts. All six paths keep separate progress and issue separate certificates; combined AI-hub progress is only a summary. Completion is learner-recorded and is not a supervised examination or identity-verified skills assessment.

How verification works

Each certificate receives an unguessable public ID, a credential ID, and a unique 64-character verification code. A public result is valid when those values match an active record in the QuickDevBase PostgreSQL database.

The verification code is a database identifier. It is not a blockchain record, digital signature, government credential, or third-party accreditation.

Privacy and withdrawal

The public page shows the chosen name, course, issue date, credential ID, and verification code. It does not show an email address. Learners can unpublish and later republish the same certificate from their account.

Corrections and issuer action

Learners can correct their displayed name. The issuer may suspend public verification for abuse, impersonation, technical error, or manipulated progress. A certificate remains subject to these terms and the privacy notice.

Independent course

This is not an Oracle, Docker, Python Software Foundation, OpenAI, Google, Anthropic, Hugging Face, or other vendor certification. QuickDevBase is independent and is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or sponsored by any referenced vendor. Vendor names and marks belong to their respective owners.
