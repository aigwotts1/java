Privacy notice

Effective 14 August 2026

This notice explains the personal data Java Basecamp uses to provide accounts, saved learning progress, and optional public certificates of completion.

Data we process

  • Your name and email address.
  • A salted password hash—never your readable password.
  • Login sessions, course progress, and completion timestamps.
  • If you choose to publish a certificate: your chosen public name, course, issue date, credential ID, and database verification code.

Why we use it

We use this information to create and secure your account, restore your progress, issue a completion record when requested, prevent abuse, and verify a certificate you voluntarily publish.

Public certificate choice

Completing the course does not automatically make your information public. A certificate becomes public only after you review the disclosure and select “Claim & publish.” Your email is never included on the public page.

You can make the certificate private again at any time. The same link will stop working publicly until you choose to republish. Updating your account name also updates the name on your certificate.

Retention and deletion

Account data remains while your account is active. Expired sessions are removed automatically. You can permanently delete your account from Profile & privacy settings; this deletes the user, sessions, progress, and certificate records through database cascade rules.

Security and service providers

Java Basecamp uses password hashing, random session tokens, HTTP-only cookies, parameterized database queries, security headers, and access controls. PostgreSQL and the deployment provider process data to operate the service. Pages currently load typefaces from Google Fonts, so Google may receive ordinary network information such as an IP address.

Your choices

You may correct your name, unpublish your certificate, or delete your account from the application. For another privacy request, contact the deployed site operator. If this project is operated from the public repository, open a repository issue without posting passwords or other sensitive information.

Java Basecamp is an independent educational project and is not affiliated with Oracle.
